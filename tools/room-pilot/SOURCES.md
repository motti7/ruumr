## Selection experiment

1. Verify public access; reject private/dead groups and replace before enabling collection.
2. Start with the four primary groups at 20 posts each, using the same date window. The collection limit is sampling, not exhaustive city coverage.
3. Compare a manually observed sample against retrieved text/links. Label offered rooms separately from searches and whole apartments; distinguish regular/sublet/unknown.
4. Test reserves only where coverage is weak. Over at least seven days, compare normalized listing content/address/price/date evidence across groups (post IDs alone miss cross-posting).
5. Keep a reserve if it adds at least 20% unique relevant offers relative to its own relevant results, or fills an otherwise missing city/language/sublet segment. Small samples stay inconclusive; do not eliminate solely from a few duplicate posts.
6. Run recurring collection only after account spend limits, source health reporting and persistent deduplication are implemented. This pilot does not yet schedule or publish.
